---
title: The Queen's Scarf
---

I am called on now to attempt a summary of what The Queen means to Rapier, as this scarf is intended to be the physical embodiment of our collective commitment. This is a task that I fear one voice, trapped in the amber of text, will fail to accomplish.

We are the Queen's army. It may be that the Queens and Roses of the Known World mean more to their rapier fighters than they themselves realize. In the days before our Peerage, they were our patrons; they were our shield. It is only fitting that now in the days of Peerage we should be their sword.

So let the scarf say to all, “There shall be friendship forever after between Atlantia's Queens, Princess, and Roses and those who seek mastery of steel: Their enemies shall be our enemies; their need shall be our need and whatever evil, or threat, or assault may come upon them, we will aid them to the utmost of our strength. This we hold so long as that which is good and green should grow on our hills, that the azure waves beat our shores, and the light of sun or star remains to guide our way.”

The Queen's Scarf was first presented to Her Majesty Genevieve at King's Assessment on July 11, 2026.  At the end of her reign the scarf was returned to the Academie and a separate stepping down gift scarf was presented to her.  Her Majesty Jan received the Queen's Scarf at Her Coronation on October 3rd, 2026.

Master Cailin Blackwood
